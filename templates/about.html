About 24 Early News

24 Early News operates as an independent digital news and wire service delivering continuous, real-time dispatches spanning global geopolitics, national governance, financial indices, bullion commodities, technology, and grassroots public affairs.

Editorial Leadership & Bureau

Founded and directed by DWIPRAJ MALLICK, the platform bridges verified institutional press feeds with disciplined journalism workflows, ensuring round-the-clock accuracy, structured reporting, and open public access to reliable information.

Core Editorial Commitments

  • Contextual Accuracy: Every dispatch correlates strictly with verified sources and context-accurate editorial imagery, avoiding sensationalism or clickbait.
  • Continuous Archiving: Breaking cables, analytical briefs, and state bulletins are indexed continuously without premature purge, serving as an enduring public record.
  • Linguistic Accessibility: Ensuring broad community reach through streamlined multi-lingual readability and rapid news delivery.

Fact-Checking & Verification Policy

Integrity is paramount to our desk. Our team cross-references breaking claims against primary official releases, accredited press releases, and established wire feeds prior to publication. Unverified user tips or breaking alerts are explicitly marked until independent verification is established.

Corrections Policy & Transparency

24 Early News is dedicated to prompt editorial accountability. When a factual error occurs, updates are issued with clear revision notices within the affected article. Readers and organizations are encouraged to notify our editorial desk of potential factual discrepancies.
