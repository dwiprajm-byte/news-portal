Effective Date: October 18, 2026

At 24 Early News (https://24earlynews.com), the privacy of our visitors is one of our primary priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by 24 Early News and how we use it.

Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to 24earlynews.com and other sites on the internet. You may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

Log Files & Advertising Partners

24 Early News follows standard log files procedures. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and clicks count.
