Privacy Policy for 24 Early News

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

At 24 Early News, accessible from 24earlynews.com and news-portal-6alq.onrender.com, the privacy of our visitors is one of our primary priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and how we utilize it.

1. Log Files and Analytical Data

Like most modern digital portals, 24 Early News follows standard log file procedures. The information gathered includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and visitor click counts to monitor platform stability and reader demographics.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

We use cookies to maintain visitors' viewing preferences and personalize user experiences across sessions. Third-party vendors may also set cookies to deliver tailored content and measure engagement.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies & Google AdSense

Google is an advertising partner on our platform. Google utilizes DART cookies to serve advertisements tailored to users visiting our site and other destinations across the web. Readers may review Google's official advertising privacy framework at Google Advertising Policies.

4. Children's Protection

We prioritize youth protection on the digital web. 24 Early News does not knowingly gather identifiable records from children under 13 years of age.

5. User Consent

By interacting with and utilizing our news service, you confirm agreement with this Privacy Policy and accept its terms.
