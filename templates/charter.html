Editorial Charter

24 Early News operates as an independent international wire service delivering continuous, real-time dispatches spanning global geopolitics, national governance, financial indices, bullion commodities, and citizen journalism.

Editorial Leadership & Bureau Governance

Founded and directed by DWIPRAJ MALLICK, the platform bridges verified institutional press feeds with disciplined citizen reporting engines, guaranteeing round-the-clock accuracy, zero duplicate clutter, and open public access.

Core Commitments

  • Zero Cross-Mapping: Every dispatch correlates strictly with verified, context-accurate press bureau imagery and authentic wire metadata.
  • 24-Hour Persistence: Live breaking cables and analytical briefs are indexed continuously without premature purge, preserving historical integrity.
  • Inclusive Translation & Accessibility: Global accessibility through automated multi-lingual language integration, ensuring zero linguistic barrier for critical updates.

Verification & Fact-Checking Standard

Our editorial desk implements rigorous double-sourcing for breaking alerts. Raw wire claims are cross-checked against official releases, gazettes, and verified ground feeds before widespread distribution.

Corrections, Retractions & Transparency

Factual accuracy takes precedence over publishing speed. When a dispatch contains an error or incomplete context, a transparent editorial notice is attached detailing the modification date and clarification.

Editorial Independence

24 Early News maintains strict editorial independence. Advertising partnerships, programmatic placements, or commercial sponsors hold no editorial influence over news judgments, story selection, or editorial investigations.
