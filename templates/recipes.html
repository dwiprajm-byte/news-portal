Authentic Shahi Mutton Dum Biryani

Prep: 25 mins | Cook: 45 mins | Servings: 4

Ingredients: 500g mutton, aged Basmati rice, saffron, ghee, whole aromatic spices, fried onions.

Technique: Marinate with yogurt and royal spices for 2 hours. Layer with semi-cooked rice and slow-cook on dum for unmatched aroma.

10-Minute High Protein Mediterranean Bowl

Prep: 10 mins | Calories: 280 kcal | Servings: 2

Ingredients: Boiled chickpeas, paneer/tofu, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, extra virgin olive oil, rock salt.

Technique: Toss fresh crisp greens and protein in extra virgin olive oil dressing for an instant energy boost.
