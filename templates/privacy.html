Statutory Transparency: Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At 24 Early News (https://24earlynews.com/), safeguarding visitor privacy is our foremost priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information recorded and how we utilize it in compliance with international data standards (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Google Publisher Policies).

1. Information Collection & Usage

We do not require mandatory user registration to browse verified news reports, market rates, or analytical features. When visitors submit community articles, culinary formulas, feedback, or editorial inquiries, details provided (such as name, email address, or verification cables) are utilized strictly for editorial authentication, correspondent communications, and fraud prevention.

2. Log Files & Automated Metrics

24 Early News follows standard automated logging protocols. Standard server log metrics (IP address, browser type, Internet Service Provider, timestamp markers, and referring/exit pages) are analyzed strictly for performance optimization, server load balancing, DDoS mitigation, and continuous syndication maintenance.

3. Cookies and Preference Settings

We utilize standard functional cookies solely to remember user-selected language preferences and prevent duplicate browser-form submissions. We do not sell or monetize personal browsing history to third-party data brokers.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Compliance

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet:

  • Advertising Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to 24 Early News and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opt-Out Rights: Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

5. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under international privacy regulations, readers possess the right to request access, correction, or erasure of any personal communication information submitted through our contact desks. Requests can be submitted directly to our compliance officer at contact@24earlynews.com.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
