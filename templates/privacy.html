Statutory Transparency: Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At 24 Early News (https://24earlynews.com/), safeguarding visitor privacy is our foremost priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information recorded and how we utilize it in compliance with international data standards (GDPR, CCPA).

1. Information Collection & Usage

We do not require mandatory user registration to browse verified news reports, market rates, or recipes. When visitors submit community articles, culinary formulas, or contact inquiries, details provided (name, email, verification cables) are utilized strictly for editorial authentication and fraud prevention.

2. Log Files & Automated Metrics

Standard automated server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp, referral pages) are analyzed strictly for performance optimization, DDoS mitigation, and continuous syndication maintenance.

3. Cookies and Preference Settings

We utilize standard functional cookies solely to remember user-selected language preferences and prevent duplicate browser-form submissions. We do not sell or monetize personal browsing history to third-party data brokers.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to our site and/or other sites across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Visitors can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

5. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

In accordance with GDPR and CCPA provisions, visitors have the right to request clarification or deletion of any correspondence data submitted to our portal by contacting us at contact@24earlynews.com.
