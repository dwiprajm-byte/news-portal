Privacy Policy

At 24 Early News, accessible from https://24earlynews.com, safeguarding the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is collected and recorded by 24 Early News and how we use it.

Log Files & Google Analytics

24 Early News follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. We use Google Analytics to analyze site traffic and improve user experience.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, 24 Early News uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.
